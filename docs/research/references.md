Additional discrete-tail reference: Rockafellar & Uryasev (2002), *Conditional Value-at-Risk for General Loss Distributions*, Journal of Banking & Finance 26(7),1443–1471, [DOI and publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0378-4266(02)00271-6). Abstract inspected, full text not inspected. Extends the definition/minimization treatment to distributions with atoms; informs fractional probability at the empirical VaR boundary. RU00's initial derivation uses continuity assumptions; a conditional mean of all observations equal to or beyond a discrete VaR is not in general the correct ES.

Bayesian posterior predictive simulation follows Gaussian conditioning above, not a separate invention. Heavy tails/tail dependence are investigated through synchronized empirical residuals and stress diagnostics; a fitted copula or multivariate t model is not justified by presently supplied data. Further full-text verification is required before relying on abstract-only results for additional algorithms.
